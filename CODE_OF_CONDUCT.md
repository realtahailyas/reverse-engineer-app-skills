# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language or imagery, or publishing other people's private information.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the people using these skills.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [Taha Ilyas](https://github.com/realtahailyas), through a GitHub private message or the contact details on his profile. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who break this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
